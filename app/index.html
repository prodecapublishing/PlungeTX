    Before you practice

    PlungeTX is a timer and a coach for practice. It is not medical advice, and it does not replace a conversation with your doctor.

    Breath holds. Sit or lie down. Never do breathwork in or near water, while driving, or while standing. Stop if you feel unwell.

    Cold. Step in slowly. Never hold your breath in the water, and have someone nearby while you are learning.

    Heat. Drink water, skip alcohol, and step out right away if you feel dizzy, sick, or too hot.

    If you are pregnant, or you have a heart, blood-pressure, or breathing condition, talk with a doctor before breath holds, cold, or heat.
