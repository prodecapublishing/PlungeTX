Common Questions

BEFORE YOU BOOK

What do I need to have ready?

A flat spot (grass or concrete both work), a standard outdoor outlet within reach, and a garden hose spigot for filling. That's it — we bring everything else. The tub is only 40 lbs empty, so if you're doing free pickup in Pearland it fits easily in most vehicles.

How cold does it get, and how long does it take?

The Pod Company Pro Chiller pulls the water down about 10°F per hour and holds it in the mid-40s even through Texas summer. Fill it in the morning and it's plunge-ready by afternoon.

Do I need to add ice?

Never. The chiller handles it — that's the whole point. No ice runs, no coolers, no melting between sessions.

How much power does it use?

Very little. The 1/2 HP chiller runs off a standard household outlet — a regular outdoor extension cord is fine. No dedicated circuit or electrician needed.

Is the water clean between users?

Yes — the system runs continuous circulation with 20-micron filtration, and we start every rental with a fresh fill.

When do drop-off and pickup happen?

We're flexible, but our usual rhythm is a Friday morning drop-off and pickup after 5PM the following Monday or Tuesday — so a "weekend" rental usually gets you a few extra days. Need different timing? Just ask.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Just text us at least 48 hours ahead and we'll reschedule or cancel — no charge, no hassle.
